How it works

From event setup to the first throw.

Seven steps, in the order you’ll do them. The app guides you through each one.

  1. 1

    Event creation

    Name it, choose the category and Singles, Doubles or Teams, set the date and the number of entries: players, pairs or teams.

  2. 2

    Format & branding

    Pick the format and best-of legs, including a knockout with Group Boards if you want a champion for every group before the finals. Add your logo, sponsors and footer. Basic setup keeps things simple; Advanced adds scheduling, per-round legs and the Plate pool.

  3. 3

    Chart details

    Add your entries. DartDraw builds balanced groups and gives each its boards, and you can adjust anything by hand.

  4. 4

    Confirm & Print Bracket

    Check each chart, confirm it, and print the blank A4 pack. Want QR codes on the sheets? Publish the results first: this step offers a button for it.

  5. 5

    Manage Tournament

    Run the day: start the games, enter scores and watch standings update. When the groups are done, draw the knockout. Share the live link so players can follow along. When the final is in, click Accept & finalize at the bottom of this step: the live page then shows “Final results”.

  6. 6

    Finalize Standings

    Review the final tables and placings. This step is read-only: to fix a score, go back to Manage Tournament.

  7. 7

    Print Final Chart

    Print the completed chart with every result, or save it as a PDF.

Good to know

Saved as you goEverything saves to your account, so you can pick up on any device.

Live pages update themselvesPlayers don’t need to refresh to see new results.

Accepted results are lockedOnce you accept them with Accept & finalize, only the Admin can reopen them.
